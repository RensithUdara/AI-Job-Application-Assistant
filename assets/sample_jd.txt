Job Title: Junior Full Stack Developer

Company: InnovateTech Solutions
Location: Remote/Hybrid
Experience Level: 1-2 years
Employment Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a talented Junior Full Stack Developer to join our growing development team. The ideal candidate will have experience with modern web technologies and a passion for building scalable web applications.

Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and maintain web applications using React.js for frontend and Node.js for backend
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features
• Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code following best practices
• Participate in code reviews and contribute to team knowledge sharing
• Work with databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB) to design and optimize data storage solutions
• Implement RESTful APIs and integrate third-party services
• Ensure responsive design and cross-browser compatibility
• Participate in agile development processes and daily standups
• Debug and troubleshoot issues across the full stack
• Stay updated with emerging technologies and industry trends

Required Skills and Qualifications:
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
• 1-2 years of professional software development experience
• Strong proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3
• Experience with React.js and modern frontend frameworks
• Knowledge of Node.js and Express.js for backend development
• Understanding of database design and SQL (PostgreSQL preferred)
• Experience with NoSQL databases (MongoDB)
• Proficiency with Git version control and GitHub
• Understanding of RESTful API design and implementation
• Knowledge of responsive web design principles
• Experience with testing frameworks (Jest, Cypress)
• Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud)
• Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices

Preferred Skills:
• Experience with TypeScript
• Knowledge of GraphQL
• Familiarity with containerization (Docker)
• Understanding of microservices architecture
• Experience with state management libraries (Redux, Context API)
• Knowledge of authentication and authorization (JWT, OAuth)
• Experience with performance optimization techniques
• Familiarity with monitoring tools and logging

Soft Skills:
• Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking abilities
• Excellent communication and collaboration skills
• Ability to work in a fast-paced, agile environment
• Self-motivated with strong attention to detail
• Eagerness to learn and adapt to new technologies
• Good time management and organizational skills

What We Offer:
• Competitive salary range: $70,000 - $85,000
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
• Flexible work arrangements (remote/hybrid)
• Professional development opportunities and training budget
• Modern development tools and equipment
• Collaborative and inclusive work culture
• Opportunities for career advancement

Application Process:
Please submit your resume, cover letter, and portfolio showcasing your best projects. Include links to GitHub repositories and any deployed applications.
